## Abstract

Investment research is a continuous process of building knowledge, interpreting new evidence, and revising judgments. Applying large language models to this process raises practical questions: How can we maintain research context, guide complex reasoning, and keep investment views current across a broad coverage universe?

Using AlphaFoundation system as a working case study, this seminar explores how to translate an analyst’s research process into connected, modular agentic workflows. The architecture rests on two foundations: company-specific “memory pods” containing accumulated facts, data, and investment judgments, and a research loop that evaluates new information and updates that knowledge.

We will walk through how the framework breaks investment research into connected tasks and workflows, exploring how these can be designed to approximate an analyst’s thinking process—from building an understanding of a business to interpreting new information, assessing its implications, and deciding when and how an investment view should change.

## About the speaker

Steven Wang is the creator of AlphaFoundation, an AI-native investment research system designed to build, maintain, and connect company knowledge through agentic workflows. His work draws on his experience as a Research Associate on the International Investment Team at Calamos Investments, where he covered companies across global markets. He focuses on translating the practical judgment and daily workflows of investment analysts into modular AI systems that support end-to-end investment intelligence operations at scale. Steven holds a master’s degree in Business Analytics from Fordham University and is pursuing a Master of Economics at the University of Sydney.
